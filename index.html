Our story

Travel with meaning.

Itihaas Gaatha is built around a simple idea: a trip should be more than reaching a destination. It should connect people with history, nature, local stories and each other.

Founded and led by Prajkta Vilas Ingle, the brand is being shaped as a friendly, responsible and experience-first travel community from Pune.

Frequently Asked Questions

Simple answers before you book your adventure.

How do I choose the right trek?

Tell us your experience level, preferred date and fitness comfort. We can recommend a suitable trip.

Can a beginner join a trek?

Yes. We will clearly mark difficulty and beginner suitability for each trip.

Can I join alone?

Yes, solo travellers can request to join available group departures.

What should I carry?

Essentials depend on the trip. A trip-specific packing list will be shared before departure.

How do I book?

Use the enquiry form below or WhatsApp us. We will confirm availability, itinerary and payment details.
